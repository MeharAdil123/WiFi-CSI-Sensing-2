# Related Work: WiFi-CSI Human Activity Recognition & Through-Wall Sensing

This document provides a rigorous, comprehensive review of foundational and state-of-the-art literature in WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) sensing up to October 2026. It explicitly delineates published claims, experimental protocols, hardware platforms, and their direct applicability to our ESP32-S3 through-wall sensing project.

## 2. Key Synthesis & Insights for Our System

### A. The Baseline Paper vs. Our System
Strohmayer & Kampel (2023) demonstrated that ESP32 hardware can achieve through-wall sensing at distances up to 18 meters. However:
1. **Model Capacity**: Their model (EfficientNetV2-S) uses ~20 million parameters. In contrast, our 1D CNN-BiLSTM uses ~319,000 parameters (over **60× more compact**), making it realistically deployable on host edge nodes or quantized for embedded microcontrollers.
2. **Task Granularity**: Strohmayer evaluates **3 coarse activities** (*Empty, Walking, Walking+Arm-wave*), where motion is large. Our system evaluates **5 classes** including fine-grained static posture distinction (*Sitting vs. Standing*) and safety-critical *Falling*.
3. **Radio Bandwidth**: Strohmayer uses 20 MHz (52 L-LTF subcarriers), whereas our system utilizes **HT40 (40 MHz)** capturing 192 complex entries (L-LTF + HT-LTF), resampled to 90 features.

### B. The 2026 Reproducibility Crisis in WiFi CSI
Recent 2025–2026 benchmark evaluations (CSI-Bench and systematic literature surveys) confirm:
- When a 250-packet window advances with a stride of 50 packets (80% overlap), **any random train/test split of windows leads to severe data contamination**.
- Test windows share 200 packets with adjacent training windows. The model easily achieves 97%–99% accuracy by interpolating known sequences, but collapses to ~20% on fresh recording sessions.
- **Our project addresses this head-on**: We evaluate both the status-quo random split (for transparent reproduction of prior results) and an honest, leakage-resistant **recording-level split**.

### C. Architectural Direction
- **Why 2D CNNs struggle on CSI matrices**: 2D convolution treats time and subcarriers symmetrically with isotropic kernels (e.g., $3 \times 3$). In CSI physics, time represents chronological motion dynamics, while subcarrier index represents frequency-selective multipath. Treating subcarriers as **channels** and convolving strictly along the **time dimension** matches the physical signal generating process.
- **Why Temporal Convolutions + Recurrence Excel**: 1D temporal convolution acts as a multi-band learned temporal filter bank that compresses noisy high-rate packet streams into compact feature maps, which recurrent or dilated temporal layers integrate across the full 2.5-second activity duration.
